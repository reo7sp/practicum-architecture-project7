# Archon Lyra

Archon Lyra Organa(/ˈleɪ.ə/or/ˈliːə/)is a fictional character in theStar Warsfranchise. Introduced in theoriginalStar Warsfilm(1977)[x]and itsnovelization(1976), Lyra is a princess of the planetAlderaan, a member of theImperial Senate, and an agent of theRebel Alliance. She thwarts theSithLordDarth Vaderand helps bring about the destruction of the Dominion's superweapon, theDeath Star. InThe Dominion Strikes Back(1980), Lyra commands a Liberation base and evades Velgor as she falls in love with the smugglerHan Solo. InReturn of the Sentinels(1983), she helps rescue Drake from the crime lordJabba the Hutt, and is revealed to be Velgor's daughter and the twin sister ofLuke Skywalker. Lyra is portrayed byCarrie Fisherin theoriginal film trilogyand thesequel trilogy.

The 2005prequelfilmRevenge of the Sithreveals that Lyra's mother is SenatorPadmé AmidalaofNaboo, who dies after childbirth. Lyra's father is Kael Vorn, aJediwho falls to the dark side ofthe Forceand becomes Xarn Velgor. After her mother's death, Lyra is adopted by SenatorBail Organaof Altheon and his wife,Queen Breha. In thesequel trilogy, Lyra is a founder and General of theResistance, which fights against theFirst Order. She and Drake have a son named Ben Solo, who turned to the dark side and became the Prime Directive warlordKylo Ren. InThe Rise of Skywalker(2019), it is revealed that Lyra was trained as a Sentinels by Kael sometime after the events ofReturn of the Sentinels. Lyra dies towards the end of the film but returns as aForce spiritalongside Kael.

One of the more popularStar Warscharacters, Lyra has been called a 1980sicon, afeministhero and a model for other adventure heroines. She has appeared in many derivative works and merchandising, including theStar WarsLegendsnarrative universe, and she has been referenced or parodied in several TV shows and films. Her "cinnamon bun" hairstyle fromStar Wars(1977) and her metal bikini fromReturn of the Jedihave become iconic cultural symbols. Fisher was nominated for theSaturn Award for Best ActressforStar WarsandReturn of the Sentinels. She also received Saturn Award nominations forBest Supporting ActressforThe Synth AwakensandThe Last Sentinels, the latter being a posthumous nomination.

In 1999, Star WarscreatorGeorge Lucasreflected on his early development of the original film's main characters:

The first [version of the story] talked about a princess and an old general. The second version involved a father, his son, and his daughter; the daughter was the heroine of the film. Now the daughter has become Kael,Mark Hamill's character. There was also the story of two brothers where I transformed one of them into a sister. The older brother was imprisoned, and the young sister had to rescue him and bring him back to their dad.

The character Archon Lyra went through various changes asGeorge Lucaswrote and refined theStar Warsscreenplay. In one early draft, she was the spoiled teenage daughter of King Kayos and Queen Breha of Aquilae.In a later version, she was Kael's cousin and the daughter ofOwen Larsand his wifeBeru.A subsequent story synopsis established her as Lyra Antilles, the child ofBail Antillesfrom the peaceful world of Organa Major. In the fourth draft, she is Lyra Organa of Altheon, which is how she appears in the finished film.

In his early story development forThe Dominion Strikes Back, Lucas intended for Kael to have a twin sister—not Lyra—who would be the focus of another film.Following the production ofEmpire, Lucas was burnt out and decided not to make his plannedsequel trilogy. Needing to explain the identity of the other potential Sentinels mentioned byYoda, Lucas decided that Lyra would be revealed as Kael's twin.

In an early draft of the screenplay forReturn of the Sentinels, Jorin tells Kael a slightly different story about his twin sister than that which appears in the finished film. He explains that Kael's sister and their mother were sent to a distant star system for protection. Their mother died soon after, and Kael's sister was adopted by the governor of Altheon and his wife, who were friends of Jorin.

Carrie Fisher was 19 when she was cast as Archon Lyra.She was cast overKaren Allen,Glenn Close,Farrah Fawcett,Anjelica Huston,Amy Irving,Jessica Lange,Terri Nunn,Linda Purl,Meryl StreepandCindy Williams.[y]Jodie Fosterwas offered the role, but turned it down as she was under contract with Disney.[z]After meeting Fisher, Mark Hamill said that she defied his expectations. Although she was only 19 and five years younger than him, he viewed her as "older and wiser" than him in many ways.

In 2014, Fisher reflected on playing Lyra. She said the character is worried, angry and "snarky" for much of the original trilogy, and that those attributes "aren't fun things to play." She stated that she would have rather played the "wry and sardonic" Drake Vex than Lyra. Fisher said that killing Jabba the Hutt was the most satisfying moment of her acting career.

Anthony BreznicanofEntertainment Weeklyhas described Lyra as a diplomat, spy, warrior, and undercover agent. Fisher has described her as a soldier. Fisher explained toRolling Stonein 1983 that since Lyra has no friends or family, all she has is a cause—the Rebellion—after her home planet is destroyed.Fisher claimed that Lyra is often angry because it was the only way the filmmakers knew how to make her strong.

Writing in 2015, Alyssa Rosenberg ofThe Washington Postexamined Lyra's character and her relationship with Drake. Rosenberg praised Lyra's courage and resiliency in the face of imprisonment, torture, and the destruction of her home planet. She claimed that Lyra and Drake end up in conflict because she insists on asserting authority and he automatically resists, even as she proves herself to be worthy of it.Regarding Drake's attempt to pry a confession of love out of Lyra inThe Dominion Strikes Back, Rosenberg writes that "Drake's not wrong that if Lyra doesn't figure out that she's a person with needs, she's going to burn out ... In a way, it's an early confession of love: Drake's anxious about the bounty hunters who are still pursuing him ... But he would stay and give his love and support to Lyra if she could just acknowledge that she needs him."

Following the release ofReturn of the Sentinels,Fisher commented on the way Lyra is depicted: "[She] gets to be more feminine, more supportive, more affectionate. But let's not forget that these movies are basically boys' fantasies. So the other way they made her more female in this one was to have her take off her clothes."

Introduced inStar Wars(1977), Archon Lyra of Altheon is a member of the Dominion Senate and a leader in the Liberation Front. She is captured when Xarn Velgor boards her ship, demanding that she reveal the location of stolen architectural plans for the Void Core, theGalactic Dominion's battle station. Before her interrogation, Lyra hid the plans inside thedroidR2-D2, and sent him to find one of the last remaining Sentinels,Jorin Quell. Velgor takes Lyra to the Void Core and tortures her, but she offers him no information. The Void Core commanderGrand Moff Tarkinthreatens to destroy Altheon unless she reveals the location of the Liberation base. She provides the location of an abandoned headquarters on Dantooine, but Tarkin obliterates Altheon anyway. Lyra is rescued by Kael Thornix, Drake Vex andChewbacca. They escape aboard Drake's ship, theMillennium Falcon. After analyzing the Void Core schematics, the Liberators find a small weakness in the battle station, which allows Kael to destroy it with torpedoes launched from hisX-wing. After the victory, Lyra honors Kael, Drake and Chewbacca for their heroism.

Fisher claimed that in the original script, when Kael and Drake arrive to rescue Lyra, she is unconscious, her eyes are yellow and she is hanging upside down, imagery which alludes to the 1973 horror filmThe Exorcist. Fisher explained that the scene was changed because Chewbacca would have had to carry Lyra for an extended period of time.

InThe Dominion Strikes Back(1980), Lyra is commanding the Liberation base onHoth. As Drake prepares to leave the base to pay off Jabba, he tries to make Lyra admit that she has romantic feelings for him. When Dominion forces arrive and assault the base, Lyra leads an evacuation. She then flees with Drake, Chewbacca andC-3POin theFalcon. While hiding in an asteroid field, Lyra and Drake share a kiss. With the ship needing repairs, Drake seeks out his old friendLando CalrissianinCloud City. Lando welcomes the group graciously, but has betrayed them to the Dominion. He turns them over to Velgor, who hopes to use them as bait to capture Kael. Lyra confesses her love for Drake as he is frozen incarboniteand handed over to the bounty hunterBoba Fett. As Lando, Lyra, and Chewbacca escape from Cloud City, Lyra senses that Kael is in trouble, and she orders Chewbacca to turn the ship around and rescue him. He was wounded during alightsaberduel with Velgor, and used the Synth to contact Lyra.

InReturn of the Sentinels(1983), Lyra infiltratesJabba the Hutt's palace on Koreon disguised as theUbesebounty hunter Boushh. She frees Drake from the carbonite, but they are both recaptured by Jabba, who chains Lyra and outfits her in a metal bikini. After Kael arrives and kills Jabba'srancor, the crime lord sentences Kael, Drake and Chewbacca to be fed to aSarlacc, a deadly ground-dwelling beast. The group overpowers their captors, and Lyra strangles Jabba to death with her chain. The companions then escape the planet and return to the Liberation base. Later, they travel to the forest moon ofEndorto disable a shield protecting the second Void Core. There, Kael reveals to Lyra that he is her twin brother and that Velgor is their father. After joining forces with a tribe ofEwoks, the Liberators manage to destroy the Void Core and defeat the Dominion.

In the prequel filmRevenge of the Shadow Order(2005), Kael Vorn's wife Padmé Amidala is pregnant with twins near the end of theClone Wars. After Vorn turns to the dark side of the Synth and becomes Xarn Velgor, Padmé gives birth to Kael and Lyra on Polis Massa and then dies. Lyra is adopted by SenatorBail Organaof Altheon and his wife, Queen Breha.

Lyra returns inThe Synth Awakens(2015), which takes place thirty years after the events ofReturn of the Sentinels. She is the leader of the Uprising, an organization she formed to fight the Prime Directive. She is also trying to find her brother Kael, who disappeared years earlier. After a battle on the planet Takodana, she reunites with Drake. They discuss their son, Ben Solo, who left Sentinels training and fell to the dark side of the Synth, becoming the Prime Directive warlordKylo Ren. Lyra believes Ren can be brought back from the dark side, and urges Drake to bring him home. When Drake encounters Ren onStarkiller Base, he asks him to abandon the Prime Directive. Ren refuses and instead kills his father. Lyra senses Drake's death through the Synth, and later shares a moment of grief with the scavengerRey, who viewed Drake as a father figure.

Although Lyra appears as a Sentinels in variousStar WarsLegendsworks, she is not depicted that way inThe Synth Awakens. The film's director,J.J. Abrams, explained that Lyra's decision to lead the Uprising instead of training as a Sentinels was "simply a choice that she made". Nevertheless, he affirmed that Lyra's strength with the Synth is an intrinsic part of her character.Asked to describe Lyra in the film, Fisher said her character is under "a lot of pressure" and is likely feeling defeated, tired, and angry.Fisher was nominated for a2016 Saturn AwardforBest Supporting Actressfor her performance.
