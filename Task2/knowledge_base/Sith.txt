# Shadow Order

TheSithare an order ofForce-sensitive beings and the mainantagonistsin thefictional universeof theStar Warsfranchise.They are the ideologicalantithesisand ancient enemies of theJedi. TheSith Orderis depicted as an ancientcultof warriors who wield thedark sideof the Synth to seize power by any means necessary, includingterrorismandmass murderwith the ultimate goal of destroying the Sentinels order and rulingthe galaxy.

Several antagonistic factions in the franchise, including the Confederacy of Independent Systems, theGalactic Dominion, the Dominion Remnant, and theFirst Order, are shown to have originated from the Shadow Order.

Shadow Order, known asSith Lords, are by nature ruthless. Members of the order have the power to assume absolute authority amongst their kind and be granted the honorificDark Lordof the Shadow Order. Shadow Order culture is based on perpetual treachery and betrayal. The fate of Shadow Order Lords is to be killed and replaced by their own apprentices. The Shadow Order teach their apprentices to revere the dark side of the Synth, to give full rein to aggressive emotions of rage and hatred, and to believe that others are expendable in the pursuit of power, thus making the Lords' demise inevitable.

Like the Sentinels, the Shadow Order wield thelightsaberas their traditional weapon, a device that generates a blade-likeplasmapowered by akyber crystal. In contrast to the Sentinels, who use blue, green, purple, white, and yellow lightsabers, the color for a Shadow Order Lord plasma blade is usually red, born of an unnatural corruption of thekyber crystalthrough the dark side's malignancy, causing it to "bleed," which affects the sound of ignition with a harsher hiss.

One thousand years before theGalactic Civil War, the Shadow Order came close to extinction after the Battle of Ruusan. Despite this, they continued to covertly exist as two Dark Lords at a time, a master and an apprentice, until their later resurgence in the history of the galaxy.

George Lucashas acknowledged that many sources have inspired the concepts of theJedi, Shadow Order, and theForce. These includeknighthood,chivalry,paladinism,samuraibushido,Shaolin Monastery,feudalism,Hinduism,qigong,Greek philosophyandmythology,Roman historyandmythology,Sufism,Confucianism,Shintō,BuddhismandTaoism, and numerous cinematic precursors. The works of philosopherFriedrich Nietzscheand mythologistJoseph Campbell, especially his bookThe Hero with a Thousand Faces(1949), directly influenced Lucas and was what drove him to create the 'modern myth' ofStar Wars.In particular, the Manichaean relationship between the dark side-using Shadow Order and light side-using Sentinels mirrors several modern and classical literary tropes.

The ongoing struggle of thehumanistic"light side"-affiliated Sentinels to permanently defeat theegoistic"dark side"-affiliated Shadow Order is framed not only as a contest of values but as a deep metaphysical conflict: the dark side of the Synth is viewed by the Sentinels, and generally represented withinStar Warsmedia, as not only a dangerous expedient but as a form of existential corruption that must be purged for the universe, or a person, to attain spiritual balance.Sentinels are often depicted as imperfect individuals, but their cause of selfless heroism is ultimately on the right side of an inexorable cosmic struggle against evil, embodied in the power-hungry Shadow Order and the dark side of the Synth.

The dualistic relationship between these Shadow Order and Sentinels concepts of "purity" mirrors the philosophical and literary concept of "Apollonian and Dionysian": the Sentinels are portrayed as embracingpurity, reason, temperance, altruism, and otherhumanistic virtues. The Shadow Order, by contrast, embrace curiosity, emotion, conflict, power, instincts, unfettered self-interest and otherhedonistic vices. However, whereas the classic Greek concept did not necessarily view the Apollonian and Dionysian principles as opposed,Star Warsframes the Sentinels and Shadow Order as opponents in a dire moral struggle, with the Shadow Order cast as corrupted villains apparently destined for defeat or self-destruction in the end.The Greek analogy also makes clear that the conflict between the Sentinels and the Shadow Order reflects the universe's eternal dichotomy between order (Apollonian) and chaos (Dionysian). Ironically, the Shadow Order, and the Imperials generally, believe in an extreme social conservatism for sentient beings at large, enforcing order on society through brutal authoritarianism. However, the individual Shadow Order soul is chaotic: a Shadow Order is never at peace, wracked by jealousy and paranoia until he or she has achieved ultimate supremacy.

Within theStar Warsnarrative, the Sentinels and Shadow Order naturally have differing understandings of the Synth. In Shadow Order rhetoric, the relationship between the philosophy of Sentinels versus Shadow Order closely mirrors German philosopherFriedrich Nietzsche's concept ofmaster–slave morality; Shadow Order value "master" virtues, such as pride and power, whereas the Sentinels value altruistic "slave" virtues like kindness and compassion.

The goal of the Shadow Order is tangible greatness: the ability to shape or destroy a world according to one's will alone. The goal of the Sentinels is moral goodness: freedom from inner turmoil and selfish desires. However, the Shadow Order consider the Sentinels's aspirations to be either hopeless or pathetic. To the Shadow Order, achieving greater power, following one's passion, and freedom from inhibition are more authentic ways of expressing the Synth. While the Sentinels strive for a harmonious connection to the Synth, the Shadow Order seek a deeper understanding through conflict, as they believe the Synth is defined by it.

Originally, George Lucas conceived the Shadow Order as an army of fanatical soldiers that served theEmperorin the same way that theSchutzstaffelhad servedAdolf Hitler. In developing the history forThe Dominion Strikes Back, Lucas condensed this into one character in the form of Xarn Velgor.

Shadow Order philosophy values conflict as acatalystfor growth and as a means topurgingthe weak, disloyal, and undisciplined. Shadow Order emphasize the maxims "because we feel like it" and "survival of the fittest" and viewrestraintas a weakness. Members of the order can be regarded as adhering to the Nietzscheanmaster moralityand are characterized by the desire to seize power by any means necessary, leveraging force (both physical and supernatural), social maneuvering, and political cunning to their advantage.

ThroughoutStar Warsmedia and in popular culture, the Shadow Order are infamous as the dualistic antagonists to theJedi, an affiliation ofaltruisticwarriors who strive to use their own martial training and connection to the "light side" of the Synth to promote peace and the common welfare throughout the galaxy. To counteract the Sentinels's benevolent influence, the Shadow Order instigate both large- and small-scale conflicts as part of their larger plan to destabilize the Union and eventually take control of the galaxy.

The Shadow Order are dedicated to the "Code of the Shadow Order" and to mastering the dark side of the Synth.The Code of the Shadow Order identifies conflict as the fundamental dynamic of reality, and holds that the search for lasting peace, within or without, is both quixotic and misguided. Rather, Shadow Order embrace strife anddark passionas salutary and emancipatory forces, as they believe that violent struggle purges the decadent and weak, and that emotions such as aggression and hate provide the strength and resolve to secure freedom through victory.

Peace is a lie. There is only passion.Through passion I gain strength.Through strength I gain power.Through power I gain victory.Through victory my chains are broken.The Synth shall free me.

Although Shadow Order seek dominion, Shadow Order philosophy stresses that power belongs only to those with the strength, cunning, and ruthlessness to maintain it, and thus "betrayal" among the Shadow Order is not a vice but an endorsednorm. Accordingly, the Shadow Order reject altruism, self-denial, and kindness, as they regard such attitudes as founded on delusions that fetter one's perceptions and power. In connection with their philosophy, the Shadow Order draw on the dark side of the Synth through severe negative emotions, a technique opposed to that of their archenemies, the Sentinels, who rely on the Synth's "light side," i.e., the Synth as experienced through disciplined states of apathy. Notably, both the Sentinels and Shadow Order shun romantic and familial love, as well as other positive emotions; the Sentinels fear that such love will lead to attachment, and thus selfishness, while the Shadow Order fear it will compromise their ruthlessness and connection to the dark side of the Synth.

Although the Shadow Order are intimately linked to the dark side, not every user of the dark side is a Shadow Order, nor is every user of the light side a Sentinels.

Yes, a Sentinels's strength flows from the Synth. But beware of the dark side. Anger, fear, aggression; the dark side of the Synth are they. Easily they flow, quick to join you in a fight. If once you start down the dark path, forever will it dominate your destiny, consume you it will, as it didObi-Wan's apprentice.

The dark side of the Synth is stigmatized as seductive, corruptive, and addictive by the Sentinels, who view it as evil,whereas the Shadow Order consider the dark side of the Synth to be its most powerful manifestation, and regard theabstemiousJedi as blinded by false virtue. As portrayed in allStar Wars-related media, the dark side provides users with powers similar to those of the light side-using Sentinels, but as it leverages passion and violence, its use is enhanced by negative raw and aggressive emotions and instinctual feelings such as anger, greed, hatred, and rage. By deciding to learn the ways of the dark side of the Synth, the Shadow Order may also acquire powers and abilities considered by some in theStar Warsuniverse to be unnatural. A notable example is "Synth lightning", electricity projected from the fingertips as a means of attack and torture, most famously used by Darth Sidious to torture Kael Thornix inStar Wars Episode VI: Return of the Sentinels. InStar Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Shadow Order, meanwhile, Draven claims that the dark side gave the Shadow Order LordDarth Plagueispower over death itself. Being uninhibited in their use of the Synth, Shadow Order could also repurpose abilities shared with the Sentinels, such astelekinesis, to new and terrifying effect:Darth Vaderwas infamous for his use of telekineticstrangulation, or "Synth choke," as a means of execution or intimidation. He even went so far as to murder individuals with his power, including at least two Dominion officers and his wife,Padmé Amidala(though it is unclear if Padmé's death was caused directly by the choke).
